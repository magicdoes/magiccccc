MAGIC SAVED ITEMS - EASY GITHUB BUILD

1. Go to github.com and create a new EMPTY repository.
2. Open the repository and choose "uploading an existing file".
3. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
4. Upload EVERYTHING inside the extracted folder, including the .github folder.
   Do not upload the ZIP itself.
5. Commit the files.
6. Open the "Actions" tab on GitHub.
7. Click "Build MagicSavedItems".
8. Click "Run workflow" -> "Run workflow".
9. Wait for the build to finish.
10. Open the completed workflow.
11. At the bottom, download the artifact named "MagicSavedItems-JAR".
12. Extract that downloaded artifact ZIP.
13. Put MagicSavedItems-1.0.0.jar into your Fabric mods folder.

You do NOT need Java or Gradle installed on your own PC.
